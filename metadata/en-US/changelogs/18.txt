* Added foreground-app-aware remapping: a button can behave differently
  depending on which app currently has focus.
* Hardened the Shizuku connection: key events arriving before the
  privileged process connects are now queued instead of dropped, and a
  dead process now triggers an automatic re-bind.

  mode, and fixed stale-process reuse on update by tying the service
  version to the app's own versionCode.

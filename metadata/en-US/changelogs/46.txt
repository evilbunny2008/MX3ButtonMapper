* Holding the MX3 remote's Home button now forwards a genuine long-press
  of Home (matching the OK button's existing hold-forwarding) instead of
  just re-running the app launch on every hold. A quick tap still opens
  MX3 Launcher directly exactly as before, even without Shizuku.
* Key press logging is now opt-in, via two new settings that are both
  off by default -- "Log every key press" (every key, from any remote)
  and "Log remapped button presses" (only what the MX3's mapped buttons
  were turned into). Turn them on only while troubleshooting.
